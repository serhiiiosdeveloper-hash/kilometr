Kilometr

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

In short
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your vehicles, fuel-ups, expenses and reminders stay on your iPhone. We don't have a server that stores them.
  • Receipts are read on your iPhone; the photos are not uploaded or kept.
  • To manage subscriptions we use Apple and our payment partner Adapty. They see your purchases, not your logbook.

1. Who is responsible

Kilometr is made by [Developer name], [Postal address] (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: support@kilometr.app.

2. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter in Kilometr is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • vehicles (make, model, year, licence plate, fuel type, tank size, odometer, optional photo);
  • fuel-ups, expenses, notes and stations you type in;
  • reminders and their dates or mileage;
  • your settings (consumption format, appearance, notification time).

We do not receive this data. It is included in your iPhone backups if you have backups turned on; Apple handles those under its own privacy policy. The Kilometr widgets read a short summary (vehicle name, odometer, consumption, next reminder) from a shared folder on the same device.

3. Camera, photos and receipts

When you scan a receipt or pick a receipt photo, the text is recognised on your iPhone with Apple's built-in technology. The image is used only to fill in the form and is not stored, uploaded or shared. Kilometr asks for camera access only to scan receipts. Photos you choose from your library (for a receipt or as a vehicle photo) are accessed one at a time through the system picker; a vehicle photo is stored on your device only.

4. Notifications

Reminder notifications are created and scheduled on your iPhone. No notification content leaves your device.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Kilometr Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or card details.

To know whether Pro is active, restore purchases and see how our plans perform, we use Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc., United States) as our processor. Adapty receives:

DataWhy
A random app-install identifier and Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV)To link your purchases to this installation and restore them
Purchase history: product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal statusTo unlock Pro and keep it in sync with the App Store
App opens and views of the Pro screenAggregated statistics about our plans
Device model, iOS version, app version, language, region and time zoneCorrect prices and troubleshooting

We have switched off the collection of the advertising identifier (IDFA) and of IP addresses. We do not send your name, email or anything from your logbook to Adapty. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in running and improving the subscription (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Adapty may process data in the United States; such transfers rely on the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Emails to support

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. The support email in the app is pre-filled with the app and iOS version to help us. We delete support conversations when they are no longer needed, at the latest two years after the last message.

7. Exporting and sharing

When you export data to CSV, the files are created on your iPhone and shared only where you send them using the iOS share sheet.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads and don't use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or rent personal data.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites (no App Tracking Transparency prompt is needed).
  • We don't access your location.

9. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it: Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. Purchase data held by Adapty is kept while it is needed to manage your subscription and as long as the law requires (for example for accounting); you can ask us to have it deleted.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Most of your data is only on your device, so you can view, export (Settings → Export to CSV) and delete it yourself. For purchase data, write to support@kilometr.app; we may need your App Store purchase receipt to find it, because we don't know who you are otherwise. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Children

Kilometr is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Security

Your data is protected by iOS security, including device encryption when your iPhone has a passcode. Communication with Apple and Adapty is encrypted.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
